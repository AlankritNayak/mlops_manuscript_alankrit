## 📄 Overview

This manuscript explores the emerging field of **Machine Learning Operations (MLOps)** — a discipline that bridges machine learning with DevOps principles to streamline the deployment, monitoring, and management of ML models in production.

The paper outlines:
- The **definition and scope** of MLOps
- How **Agile methodologies** enhance ML and big data project management
- The **lifecycle and tooling** of MLOps (e.g., Kubeflow, MLflow)
- Key challenges: technical, organizational, operational, and ethical
- Comparison between **DevOps and MLOps**
- Emphasis on **Explainability, Fairness, Accountability, and Sustainability**

## 📌 Key Topics

- 🚀 MLOps Workflow and Automation  
- 🧠 Agile Methods in ML Project Management  
- ⚖️ Ethical Foundations: Fairness, Explainability, Responsibility  
- 🧰 Tools: Kubeflow, MLflow, CD4ML  
- 🏗 System Architecture & Understandability (UML diagrams)  
- 🌐 Multi-organization Collaboration Challenges  
- 📊 Model Lifecycle: Training, Validation, Monitoring  
- ⚙️ Integration, Governance, and Continuous Delivery

## ✅ Conclusion

To realize the full potential of AI systems, organizations must adopt MLOps practices that are sustainable, transparent, and well-governed. This manuscript provides foundational guidance for both academic and industry professionals in managing ML workflows efficiently.
